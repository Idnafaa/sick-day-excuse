One line, 112 characters, that talks its way out of work: a pulled muscle,
a snooze button, and a workplace-injury claim filed before clocking in.
Built for make.fun's "The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse" bounty ($2 in USDC).

## What it does

`python3 main.py` writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt` — a single line,
no trailing newline, nothing else.

## The line

> I pulled a muscle reaching for the snooze button, so technically I got injured at work before I even clocked in.
